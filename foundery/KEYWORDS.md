# Foundery keyword bank (audience + topic)

Use for: captions, on-screen hooks, bio/profile text, hashtags, comment-to-DM triggers, and search/SEO titles.
Rule of thumb: hook and caption language speaks to the *buyer's pain* (buyer terms); hashtags mix a few broad + several niche.

## 1. Who we talk to (audience terms)
business owner, small business owner, founder, solopreneur, entrepreneur, operator, CEO, COO,
agency owner, marketing agency owner, lead gen agency owner, SMMA owner, consultancy owner,
real estate agent / broker / team lead, recruiter / staffing agency owner, hotel / hospitality owner,
clinic / dental practice owner, coach / consultant, e-commerce store owner, service business owner,
sales team lead, ops manager, head of growth

## 2. Their pain (hook + caption language)
manual work, repetitive tasks, busywork, copy-paste work, data entry, admin overload,
drowning in follow-ups, missed calls, missed leads, slow lead response, leads going cold,
no time, can't scale, hiring more people to scale, payroll for repeat tasks, too many tools,
spreadsheets everywhere, inbox overload, client reporting takes hours, onboarding takes forever,
chasing invoices, no-shows, human error, burnout, bottlenecked on me

## 3. What we sell (solution terms)
business automation, AI automation, workflow automation, process automation, automate my business,
AI agent, AI assistant, AI receptionist, AI voice agent, AI chatbot, lead capture automation,
lead follow-up automation, CRM automation, email automation, appointment booking automation,
missed call text-back, speed to lead, lead qualification, lead nurturing, sales automation,
reporting automation, client onboarding automation, invoice automation, no-code automation,
done-for-you automation, AI systems, systems and processes, Zapier, Make (make.com), n8n, GoHighLevel,
HubSpot, Zoho, Airtable, Notion, Google Sheets, Slack, Gmail, Twilio, OpenAI, Claude

## 4. Niche-specific pairs (agency + automation)
- Marketing agencies: agency automation, client reporting automation, campaign reporting, lead handoff, agency workflows, agency scaling, white-label automation
- Lead gen companies: lead gen automation, lead routing, lead scoring, cold outreach automation, appointment setting, speed to lead, lead follow-up sequence
- Real estate: real estate lead follow-up, missed call text-back, showing booking, buyer/seller lead nurture
- Recruiting: candidate screening automation, resume parsing, interview scheduling, staffing agency automation
- Hospitality: booking automation, after-hours booking, guest messaging, review requests
- Local services: quote follow-up, review generation, reactivation campaigns, dispatch and scheduling

## 5. Outcome terms (proof language)
save time, save hours a week, cut costs, reduce headcount cost, scale without hiring, 24/7,
respond in seconds, book more calls, more booked appointments, faster lead response, more revenue, fewer no-shows,
run on autopilot, hands-off, "goes automatic"
