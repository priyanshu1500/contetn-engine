## 2. The 4 Fundamental Typography Pillars

### Pillar 1: The Narrative Sans (Conversational Flow)
- **Family:** `Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, `-apple-system`.
- **Styling:** Weight 500–600, letter-spacing -0.5px.
- **Rule:** Used for 70% of standard script words. Keep it humble, clean, and legible.

### Pillar 2: The Editorial Serif Italic (Emotional Punch)
- **Family:** `Playfair Display`, `Newsreader`.
- **Styling:** Weight 700, Italic, letter-spacing +0.5px.
- **Rule:** Reserved exclusively for words carrying emotional weight, pedigree, obsession, or high status.
- **Words to trigger:** *obsession*, *genius*, *unhinged*, *pedigree*, *conviction*, *legendary*, *brutal*, *masterpiece*.

### Pillar 3: The Condensed Display (Hard Metrics & Numbers)
- **Family:** `Bebas Neue`, `Anton`, `Druk Wide`.
- **Styling:** Weight 800–900, uppercase, letter-spacing +1.5px.
- **Rule:** Triggered on all numerical values, monetary amounts, and batch indicators.
- **Words to trigger:** `$40M`, `70 TAKES`, `$500B`, `16-HOUR`, `DAY ONE`.

### Pillar 4: The Monospace Archival Stamp (Technical Proof)
- **Family:** `IBM Plex Mono`, `Space Mono`.
- **Styling:** Weight 600, uppercase, letter-spacing +2px.
- **Rule:** Used for dates, timestamps, code filenames, and database counters.

## 3. Physical Asset Construction Rules

### A. The Taped Polaroid Card
When displaying founder portraits or historical photos:
1. **Container:** White border card (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle shadow: `boxShadow: '0 20px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.45)'`.
2. **Aspect Ratio:** Square or 4:5 photo area with generous bottom border (40px) for handwriting.
3. **The Masking Tape:** Semi-transparent cream bar (`rgba(240, 235, 220, 0.85)`) pinned diagonally across the top edge.
4. **The Label:** Handwritten cursive (`Caveat`) font slightly angled (-2deg to +3deg).

### B. The Document Highlighter
When highlighting proof (YC email, SEC filing, Reddit 2005 post):
1. **Background:** Authentic scanned texture or ultra-clean modern UI screenshot.
2. **Highlighter Box:** `background: rgba(245, 197, 66, 0.45)` with `mixBlendMode: 'multiply'`.
3. **Animation:** Width expands from `0%` to `100%` over 12 frames (0.5s) using `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`.
4. **Audio Sync:** Trigger `marker_squeak.wav` at start frame of highlight.
