Caption & pinned comment

Caption

Case File No. 001 — the 9-pointer nobody called back. Fourth year. Non-NLU. 8.0. Three publications. Real internships. Zero interviews — while classmates with thinner CVs got Tier-1. His CV wasn't weak. It just never turned any of it into a hiring signal. Every internship line said "assisted" or "observed". The publications were student-portal posts. Nothing said what kind of law he wanted to practise. Comment FILE and I'll send you the full breakdown + the annotated rewrite.

Pinned comment

The fix wasn't more experience — it was language. "Assisted on research" → "drafted 3 sections of a writ petition, cited in the final filing." Same work. One sends a signal. Comment FILE for the line-by-line.
