Dossier 01

Executive read

Three findings that set the strategy

Law-career Instagram is retention-poor. The largest dedicated account posts multiple times a day and still draws single- and double-digit likes on most posts. The account that is working — @traceyourcase, 90k, a founder-creator with LexIntent’s exact positioning — got there on 291 posts, not thousands. Lean and engineered beats high-volume and generic.

Second: the one growth mechanic working in this niche right now is “comment [keyword] → I DM you a resource.” It is not subtle and it is not new, but it is producing 1,000–5,800 comments on single reels while the same accounts’ likes sit in the hundreds. Even the AI-editorial reference creators run the identical loop.

Third: the audience’s fear is specific, verbatim, and repeats across five years of forum threads. It is not “career anxiety.” It is “my peers with worse CVs are getting Tier-1 and I can’t get past stage one.” That sentence is a content series.

Margin note —

The lane LexIntent wants is empty. Nobody is producing Economist-grade motion for the law-student CV grind. Retention-first editorial reels + the comment-to-DM loop + these exact fears = the opening.

Phase 1 · The mechanic

One growth loop is doing all the work

Comment-to-DM. A reel ends with “comment BANK and I’ll send you the list.” Every comment is a reply notification, a re-surface, and a DM opened. Three independent confirmations in this niche: @legalshotswithvakeel runs 5,813 comments through it on a single reel; @gregisenberg runs the identical structure with “comment cloudflare, follow me”; @traceyourcase — the closest model to LexIntent — pulled 2,080 comments with “comment judge” on a plain photo, no reel required.

For LexIntent this is the primary CTA, and it fits the brand’s “make people want the next edition” rule: the resource is always a teardown, checklist, or the next Dispatch — never a pitch. The product enters only when someone replies to the DM.

Instagram comments are not machine-readable through the current toolset, so this draws on Legally India convo threads (each titled by the fear, with verbatim member posts), LinkedIn, and press — triangulated against the earlier Reddit/Quora pass in project memory. Ranked by how often the theme recurs.

  1. “Peers with worse CVs are getting Tier-1. Mine gets rejected at stage one.” “my peers are getting in tier 1 firms with a resume worse than mine… I just want a chance to prove that I am a perfect candidate but the HRs keep rejecting my application.” — Legally India, thread #185010 Emotion: injustice + helplessness. → Resume Autopsy / Hidden Signal series.
  2. “No contacts. The placement cell is a nightmare.” “I feel anxious about the future that I’ll get unemployed because I have no contact and our college placement cell is a nightmare.” — same thread Emotion: isolation, fixed fate. → Recruiter’s Desk — “how the shortlist actually gets made.”
  3. Non-NLU disadvantage Recurring: “how to get an assessment internship at a Tier-1 firm from a non-NLU” — multiple Legally India threads. Emotion: the ceiling is set by the college name. → counter-evidence content; case files of non-NLU callbacks.
  4. Corporate vs litigation paralysis Three separate 2026 career-guide pages (Lawsikho, iPleaders, Lawspot) rank for “litigation or corporate law” — sustained, unresolved demand. Emotion: fear of the irreversible first move. → the Corporate vs Litigation reel already drafted.
  5. “I wish someone had told me before my first internship.” LinkedIn posts phrased in exactly these words, high engagement. Emotion: preventable regret. → insider-knowledge series, first-person framing.
  6. The employability gap (macro proof) “India’s legal sector is booming — so why are 98% of law grads left out of the top jobs?” — Mint, Jun 2026 Not a fear, a hook stat. Opens any “the system, explained” reel.

The gate

Every reel clears three filters or it doesn’t ship

  1. Would someone stop scrolling inside three seconds? — the hook creates an information gap, not a topic announcement.
  2. Can it be produced in under 20 minutes once the templates exist? — if it needs a new animation, it belongs in Phase 4, not the calendar.
  3. Does it read as investigative editorial — a leaked case file — rather than another AI page? Newspaper type, red-pen marks, paper motion, evidence before opinion.
