The gap isn't animation quality. It's psychological pacing: no warm-up, the hook promising the whole story, a meaningful change every 2–3s, captions as a visual layer, and payoffs on a schedule instead of one long explanation.

01 The seven fixes

1 — Rebuild the hook 0–3s · highest leverage

Now: white ground, calm type, eases into "Case File No. 001". A warm-up.
To: four cuts, four seconds, the whole story stated before any context:
Same GPA.
Same law school.
One got Khaitan.
One got ignored.
How: 4 kinetic-Anton beats @ ~0.8s each, hard cuts, each with a paper-thud SFX. The masthead / CASE FILE card moves to a 1-second cold-open before this, not a 4-second reveal.

2 — Make every animation informational 3–8s

Now: circles animate nicely but decoratively — "something moved".
To: every motion answers "what changed?" Forensic, not pretty.
Add: a red scan line sweeping the CV, a cursor that travels to each weak phrase, a ticking timer (7→0), a recruiter eye-path dotted line. Motion reveals information.

3 — The document is the actor 18–30s · the plateau

Now: the rewrite is told ("better wording") over static text. Retention dips here.
To: the resume edits itself live on screen.
Show: words strike through, a red arrow points, a margin note writes itself in Caveat, the fixed sentence highlights. Compress the lesson to three insights in six seconds:
Weak verb.
No outcome.
No drafting signal.

4 — Payoffs on a schedule whole reel

Now: one long payoff near the end.
To: a concrete reward every 5–10 seconds — retention stepping-stones.
TimeReward
2sSurprise — the two-outcome contrast
7sRule — "the first 7 seconds decide everything"
13sResume scan — the red line finds the weak lines
20sBefore / after — the line rewritten, meter jumps
30sHidden signal — the pattern named
40sFinal insight + next-case tease

5 — Louder headlines pattern interrupts

Now: "What they read first." — premium, but quiet.
To: oversized headline moments that stop the scroll.
THE FIRST 7 SECONDS
DECIDE EVERYTHING
How: full-bleed Anton, 200–260px, oxblood underline drawing under it, paper-grain behind. 2–3 of these per reel as hard visual beats.

6 — Captions as a primary layer the missing retention driver

Now: no burned-in captions at all. 80%+ watch on mute.
To: word-level "karaoke" captions, 2–3 words at a time, bottom third, the key word flipping to oxblood as it's spoken.
How: faster-whisper on our own vo.mp3 → word timestamps → a caption track in the composition. Big Anton/Inter, hard-shadow, safe margins. This alone typically moves retention several points.

7 — Series framing + unfinished loop 30–45s

Now: ending is elegant but safe ("Not a better resume. The right one.").
To: land on a bigger implication / identity shift, then open a loop.

02 The flagship format — locked

Stop making beautiful standalones. Every episode is the same skeleton; only the evidence changes. Repeatable, bingeable, recognisable in three frames, and easy to assemble.

0–2s
CASE FILE 00N — cold open. Number + STATUS: REJECTED stamp thud. payoff
2–6s
Hook — 4 kinetic cuts, the whole story. payoff
6–12s
Evidence — the CV enters as an exhibit; scan line + cursor find the weak lines. payoff
12–20s
Recruiter scan — 7-second timer, eye-path, signal meter stays flat. payoff
20–32s
Redline replay — the document edits itself live; meter jumps on the rewrite. payoff
32–42s
Verdict — bordered card, FILED stamp, the one-line implication. payoff
42–45s
Next case tease + comment-to-DM CTA. Unfinished loop.

This is close to what v2 already does — the change is sharper hook, informational motion, live editing, louder headlines, captions, and the tease. Same render pipeline.

03 The recurring signature

Three elements, every reel, no exceptions — recognisable as LexIntent within three frames:

Burgundy red-pen circle — rough hand-drawn ellipse, DrawSVG stroke-on, always oxblood #7a1712
Handwritten margin note — Caveat, slight rotation, writes in beside the circled thing
Physical paper movement — every scene change is a sheet sliding / a page turning, never a fade

Plus the locked cold-open card (CASE FILE 00N / STATUS) and the two stamps (REJECTED red, FILED oxblood).

04 Software & integrations to add

Captions — faster-whisper · free · pip · CPU

  • Runs on our own vo.mp3, returns word-level timestamps in seconds.
  • Feeds a karaoke caption track (2–3 words, key word → oxblood). The single biggest retention upgrade for mute viewers.
  • OpenChatCut also ships a whisper-cli.exe + has a built-in caption panel — usable if we build in OCC.

Voice — Sarvam Bulbul v3 · free 10k/mo + ₹1,000 credit · then ₹30/10k

  • India-built, 35+ genuinely-Indian voices. Wire into narrate.py.
  • Fallback: ElevenLabs Starter $5/mo (best quality + voice clone). Kokoro (now installed locally, needs a 350 MB model) is the free-local option.

Beat map — faster-whisper + librosa / aubio · free · pip

  • Detect the music bed's transients → snap every cut, headline and caption reveal to the grid. This is what makes elite reels feel "locked".

Micro-animations — Lottie · free tier · @remotion/lottie or inline

  • Polished pre-made arrows, checkmarks, loaders, redaction reveals for the forensic layer — faster than hand-animating each one.

Illustration layer — DiceBear API + Humaaans · CC0 / MIT · free

  • For the reference-2 opening look — flat characters recoloured to tokens, parallax depth. (Detailed in the system plan.)

DaVinci Resolve · free · optional NLE

  • If you ever want a real timeline for final colour, caption polish, or Fusion motion graphics on top of the HyperFrames render. Not required — nice to have.

Phase 2 — Blender · free · deferred

  • Real 3D depth for the "evidence on a desk" look (Greg-tier). Blocked on RAM/time today; revisit after the format is proven.

05 Motion library — build once, reuse

Eight components, authored as HyperFrames + OpenChatCut modules. After this, a reel is assembly.

Paper slide — sheet enters/exits with a cast shadow; the default transition
Red-pen draw — DrawSVG rough ellipse / underline / strike, oxblood
Page flip — 3D-ish corner lift between exhibits
Ink spread — a stamp or highlight blooming into place
Rubber stamp — scale-overshoot + settle + thud (REJECTED / FILED)
Cursor highlight — a pointer travelling a path, pausing to select text
Score / signal counter — the meter + a numeric tick
Redaction bar — black bar wipes on, then retracts to reveal

06 Sound — feedback on every transition

Top documentary reels use tiny sounds constantly. Rule: no cut without a sound.

  • Per-transition: paper rustle · pen scratch · page flip · stamp thud · subtle sub-bass.
  • A continuous low room-tone bed under everything so silence never feels dead.
  • Source: local video-shotcraft pack (154 SFX) first; Freesound CC0 for gaps. All beat-mapped.

07 The Asset Sweep — locked per-reel rule

Rule: no reel gets built without a fresh asset sweep first. Every episode begins by pulling topic-matched b-roll, textures, stills and archival footage from the free libraries, then using everything that reads aesthetic and on-brand to lift the production feel above pure motion-graphics.

The sweep — runs at the start of every reel

1
Derive queries from the script. 6–10 phrases from the hook + evidence + verdict — e.g. "law library", "signing contract", "rejection letter", "typewriter close up", "courthouse steps", "hands sorting paper".
2
Pull from every free source via pull_assets.py (topic mode): Pexels + Pixabay video & photo, Prelinger / Internet Archive PD film, Openverse CC0, The Met CC0, Coverr, Mixkit. ~3–4 candidates per query.
3
Curate on a contact sheet. Keep only clips/stills that: match the warm-cream/oxblood palette or take a grade to get there; are slow / textural / documentary (no stocky smiles, no logos, no motion that fights the type); add atmosphere behind headlines, under paper, or as an exhibit.
4
Grade to brand. Desaturate ~40–70%, warm the whites toward #f3efe4, crush blacks slightly, add grain. B-roll usually rides at 8–18% opacity behind type or full-bleed for a 1–2s texture beat — never as the literal subject.
5
Place — one atmospheric clip behind the cold-open and the verdict; archival stills as physical evidence on the desk; a paper/ink texture layer on every card; a grain-video overlay on the whole reel at ~8%.
6
Log every file used in assets\LICENSES.csv (CC0 / free-commercial only — nothing non-commercial).

Already wired: Pexels + Pixabay API keys in assets\.env, pull_assets.py and pull_prelinger.py in the scratchpad, ~150 clips/stills already banked in D:\agency content\assets\. This step adds a topic mode so the pull is scoped to each reel, plus the contact-sheet + grade pass.
